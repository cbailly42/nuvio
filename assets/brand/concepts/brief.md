# Design brief: Nuvio

**Summary**: Nuvio is a glass-style Home Assistant dashboard (one frontend plugin, installed via HACS) that builds
itself from your home's areas, devices and entities, then lets you rearrange everything in place.

**Audience**: Home Assistant users (hobbyists and their households). They meet the mark in the HACS list, the
GitHub README, the HA sidebar/browser tab (16 to 32 px), the first-run wizard and on phones as a home-screen icon.
Secondary viewers: family members who only see the wall tablet.

**Brand adjectives**: calm, safe, quiet, homely, trustworthy.
**Avoid feeling**: technical, alarmist, gadgety.

**Promise / big idea**: Your home, quietly in order. Open it and everything is where it should be.

**Competitive landscape**: Home Assistant's own mark (blue house with circuit nodes) dominates the category, and
dashboards/integrations copy it: house outlines, wifi arcs, shields, circuit dots, light bulbs, gauges, tile grids
(MDI `view-dashboard`). Open territory: soft, human, low-contrast forms; a single quiet idea instead of a gadget.

**Off-limits clichés** (unless genuinely reworked): house outline/roof, wifi arcs, shields/padlocks, circuit nodes,
light bulbs, gauges, 2x2 tile grid, HA blue.

**Mark type hypothesis**: a symbol is required (favicon, app icon, HA sidebar). Explore a letterform (n), a
monogram (nu) and an abstract/pictorial symbol, each paired with a lowercase wordmark.

**Must work on**: 16 px favicon, HA sidebar icon, HACS listing, iOS/Android home-screen icon, README header in light
and dark mode, one colour (black/white), lime accent surfaces.

**Constraints / equity**: product accent is lime `#63D71E` (text on lime is black), soft radii (24 / 16 px tokens),
glassmorphism UI. The current logo (lime gradient ribbon "N" over a rounded "nuvio") sets a lowercase, rounded
precedent; no obligation to keep the ribbon.

**Success criteria**: recognisable at 16 px; reads as calm and safe before it reads as "tech"; clearly distinct
from the Home Assistant logo; works in one colour; one idea, explainable in one sentence.

**Decision-maker**: project owner (Krzysztof Czepiec). **Deliverables now**: three concepts + concept sheet.
Kit (favicon, app icon, one-colour set, usage guide) only after a direction is chosen.

**Assumptions I made** (fast track, no questions asked):
- The mark is a new symbol + lowercase wordmark; the existing ribbon N is not sacred.
- Lime stays the brand colour; concepts are shown in greyscale first.
- Wordmark letters are constructed as paths (no font licence needed); a final type study comes with the kit.
- English/Polish audience; no wording in the symbol.
- No trademark search was done; a professional search is recommended before adoption.

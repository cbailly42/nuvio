Nuvio logo usage

One page for anyone placing the logo: README, HACS, docs, the dashboard, slides, print.

1 · The logo

A lowercase n drawn as an arched doorway with the door inside: shelter, home, quiet order. On dark backgrounds the door lights up lime, a light left on.

2 · Clear space and proportions

d = the width of the door (one quarter of the symbol's width). Keep 1 d clear on every side of every version. Horizontal: symbol to wordmark gap = 1 d. Stacked: symbol base to i-dot = 1 d. Wordmark x-height = 0.48 × symbol height. Use the supplied lockups; never rebuild them.

5 · Don'ts

Don't stretch or squash
Don't recolour outside the palette
No lime or white logo on light (1.9 : 1)
No shadows, gradients or glass effects
Don't rearrange or resize lockup parts
Nuvio
Don't retype the wordmark in a font
Typography. The wordmark is custom geometry (outlined paths). Supporting text: DM Sans (SIL Open Font License, the dashboard's UI font), 400/500/600; fallback system-ui.
Files. assets/brand/final/ (SVG masters, png/), icons/ (favicon, app and web icons). Questions: the Nuvio maintainer. No trademark search done yet.

# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Nuvio to be a friendly and welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient with other people
- respecting different opinions and experiences
- giving and accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or images and unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insults and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing other people's private information without their permission
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for this code and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow it. Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through a [private report on GitHub](https://github.com/kczepiec/nuvio/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@kczepiec](https://github.com/kczepiec). Every report will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

Depending on the situation, the maintainer may give a private warning, ask for a public apology, or ban someone from the project for a time or permanently.
